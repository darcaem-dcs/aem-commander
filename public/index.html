CSAR Module

Spawns downed pilots when aircraft are destroyed, allowing human players to fly search and rescue missions. You must define in the mission editor late activation groups for ground and naval spawns for both coalitions.

Distress signal that will be transmited through a random frequency and can be used to home in on the location of downed pilots.

Civilian Traffic Module

Generates random background civilian air traffic between designated airbases and zones to bring the world to life. NOTE: this is designed for airliners, so it uses "OpenBig" parking spots. You can use the Civilian Aircraft Mod (not afiliated, just sharing the link).

Group name of the late activation groups that will be used randomly to generate civilian aircraft with different liveries.

You can define zones in the mission editor that will also be used for the civilian traffic to spawn and/or be directed to.

Every civilian flight will be assigned a flight plan greater that this value.

Number of civilian flights to spawn at the start of the mission, and maximum alive at the same time.

Where the civilian aircraft can spawn and be directed to.

QRA Interception Module

Triggers Quick Reaction Alerts by spawning random unidentified aircraft that must be intercepted and warned off via radio.
